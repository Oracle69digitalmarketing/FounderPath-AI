# How to Use FounderPath AI

Welcome to your Agentic Economic GPS. Follow this guide to transform your skills into a professional business venture in under 10 minutes.

## Step 1: The Prosperity Onboarding
1. Click **"Get Started"** on the landing page.
2. Sign in with your Google Account. This creates your secure **Prosperity Profile** where your blueprints will be stored.

## Step 2: The Skill Audit (SkillThinker™)
1. On your Dashboard, find the **"Auditing Your Human Capital"** panel.
2. Describe your skills, tools, and experience in plain English.
   * *Example: "I am a trained tailor with an industrial sewing machine and I live in a busy area of Lagos."*
3. **Discovery Loop**: If the AI needs more details, it will ask you 3 follow-up questions. Answer these clearly to ensure your business plan is highly specific.

## Step 3: Review Your Venture Blueprint
Once the analysis is complete, your **Master Recommendation** will appear. Explore the tabs:
- 📊 **Strategy**: View your revenue model and Go-To-Market strategy.
- 📋 **Business Plan**: Read a 250+ word Executive Summary, detailed Market Analysis, and 10-day roadmap.
- 🎴 **Pitch Deck**: View the 7 slides you'll need to present to partners or investors.
- 🔍 **Market Scout**: Get specific networking scripts and search terms for Facebook and LinkedIn.
- 🎓 **Learning Hub**: Access curated links to master the gaps in your venture readiness.

## Step 4: Professional Presentation
Click the **"Download Business Plan"** button. This generates a professionally formatted `.txt` document including:
- Confidentiality markings.
- Itemized Startup Budgets in Naira (₦).
- Strategic Risk Mitigation tables.
- Day-by-day launch sequences.
*This document is ready to be shared with banks, partners, or local business registries.*

## Step 5: The 10-Day Launch
1. Scroll to the bottom of your recommendation.
2. Click **"Launch in 10 Days"**.
3. Provide your **WhatsApp number**.
4. The system will initiate your launch sequence and provide immediate "Nudges" in your Dashboard Nudge Center to keep you on schedule.

---
**Founder Tip:** Be as descriptive as possible during the Skill Audit. The more context you give about your location and specific tools, the more research-backed and viable your business plan will be!
